# Cracked or bad eggs

- Check your eggs when the rider hands them over.
- Report cracked eggs within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo, by message or email.
- We replace every cracked egg free of charge on your next delivery, or refund it if you prefer.
- Bad or spoiled eggs (bad smell, unusual colour inside): report within 7 days of delivery, with a photo. We replace them free of charge.
- Pugad Assist cannot approve a replacement or refund itself; it only explains the steps. Our staff handles each report.
