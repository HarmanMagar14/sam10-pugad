# How to order

1. Message our order line or email us (see Hours and contact). Pugad Assist cannot take orders.
2. Tell us: your name, delivery address, egg size, number of trays or dozens, and preferred delivery day.
3. Our staff replies with the total and the delivery schedule. Your order is confirmed only after staff confirms it.

- Minimum order for delivery: 3 trays (any mix of sizes), or 2 trays if you pick up at the Pugad Hub.
- Cut-off: orders confirmed before 3:00 PM are delivered the next delivery day.
- Changing or cancelling an order: contact staff before 6:00 PM the day before delivery.
