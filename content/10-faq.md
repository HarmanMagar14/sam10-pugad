# Frequently asked questions

**What is the cheapest egg size?** Peewee, at PHP 180 per tray of 30.

**Can I mix sizes in one order?** Yes. The 3-tray minimum counts all sizes together.

**Can I buy a single egg or a half tray?** No. We sell by the tray (30) or by the dozen (12).

**Do you deliver on weekends?** No. Delivery days are Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

**Are your eggs organic?** No. They are regular farm eggs from partner farms.

**Do you sell brown eggs?** Our eggs are a mix of brown and white. You cannot choose the colour.

**Can I return eggs I don't need?** No. Only cracked or bad eggs are replaced (see the cracked eggs policy).

**Can Pugad Assist place my order?** No. Pugad Assist only answers questions. Our staff takes and confirms orders.

**Do you sell chicken feed or live chickens?** No. We only sell chicken eggs.

**Is Pugad Fresh Eggs real?** No. It is an invented business for a school project, and all details here are made up.
