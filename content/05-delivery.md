# Delivery

- Areas: Angeles City and Mabalacat City only.
- Delivery days: Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
- Delivery time: between 8:00 AM and 12:00 noon.
- Delivery fee: PHP 60 per drop-off.
- Free delivery: orders of 10 trays or more.
- Outside our areas (for example San Fernando, Porac or Clark Freeport): we do not deliver yet. You can pick up at the Pugad Hub.
- If you are not home: the rider waits 10 minutes and calls you once, then the order is rescheduled to the next delivery day.
