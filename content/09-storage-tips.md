# Storing your eggs

- Store eggs in the refrigerator, pointed end down, in their tray.
- In the refrigerator, our eggs stay fresh for about 4 weeks from delivery.
- At room temperature in a cool, shaded place, use them within 7 days.
- Do not wash eggs before storing. Wash them just before cooking.
- Float test: put an egg in a bowl of water. If it sinks and lies flat, it is fresh. If it stands up, use it soon. If it floats, throw it away.
