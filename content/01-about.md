# About Pugad Fresh Eggs

Pugad Fresh Eggs is an invented egg supplier used for a school project (SAM10). It is not a real business.

- What we sell: fresh chicken eggs, sorted by size, sold by the tray (30 eggs) or by the dozen.
- Where we deliver: Angeles City and Mabalacat City, Pampanga.
- Who we serve: households, sari-sari stores, bakeries and carinderias.
- Our promise: eggs are collected from partner farms and delivered within 3 days of collection.
- We do NOT sell: chicken, chicks, duck eggs, quail eggs, balut, salted eggs, chicken feed or farm supplies.
