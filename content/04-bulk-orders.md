# Bulk orders (bakeries, carinderias, stores)

A bulk order is 20 trays or more in one delivery.

- 20-49 trays: PHP 10 off per tray.
- 50 trays or more: PHP 15 off per tray.
- Bulk discounts follow these fixed rules only. Pugad Assist cannot give, change or promise special discounts or credit terms for any customer. Ask our staff.
- Standing weekly orders are available for bulk customers. Arrange them with our staff.
- To request a bulk quote, email us with the sizes, number of trays and how often you need them.
