# Egg sizes and prices

All prices are in Philippine pesos (PHP), include VAT, and are valid until further notice.
One tray holds 30 eggs. One dozen is 12 eggs.

| Size | Weight per egg | Price per tray (30) | Price per dozen (12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peewee | under 45 g | PHP 180 | PHP 78 |
| Small | 45-50 g | PHP 200 | PHP 86 |
| Medium | 50-55 g | PHP 225 | PHP 96 |
| Large | 55-60 g | PHP 250 | PHP 106 |
| Extra large | 60-65 g | PHP 270 | PHP 114 |
| Jumbo | over 65 g | PHP 295 | PHP 124 |

Prices can change with farm supply. The price that applies is the one confirmed when the order is accepted by our staff.
