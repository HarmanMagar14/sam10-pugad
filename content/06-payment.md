# Payment

- Accepted: cash on delivery (COD) and GCash.
- We do not accept credit cards, cheques or bank transfers.
- GCash: pay only after our staff confirms your order total. Send the reference number in the same chat.
- Bulk customers with a standing weekly order pay on each delivery day.
- Never send card numbers, passwords or one-time PINs to Pugad Assist or anyone claiming to be Pugad staff.
