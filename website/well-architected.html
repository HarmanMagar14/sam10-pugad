Gap to fix first: Reliability — the single server

Problem. Everything (web server, app, database) is on one EC2 instance. If it fails, customers get nothing until a person notices, logs in and rebuilds it by hand, and the newest data is only as fresh as last night's backup.

Why first. It is the only failure that takes the whole service down and loses data. The alarm pugad-instance-unhealthy already detects it; nothing acts on it yet.

Fix. (1) Add the EC2 recover action to pugad-instance-unhealthy so AWS moves the instance to healthy hardware automatically. (2) Turn on daily EBS snapshots with Data Lifecycle Manager so a whole-server rebuild takes minutes, not an afternoon. (3) Move backups from nightly to every 6 hours to cut worst-case data loss from 24 h to 6 h. All three cost cents a month.
